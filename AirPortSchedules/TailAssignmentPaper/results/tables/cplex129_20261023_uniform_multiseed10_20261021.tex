\subsubsection{CPLEX replication of the ten-seed uniform study}
\label{subsec:cplex_refresh}
The same 40 inputs were rerun with CPLEX~12.9.0.0 through Pyomo's
shell interface, one thread, presolve enabled, no warm start, and a
180-second limit per solve. The defaults of this CPLEX version were
retained, including its MIP-gap tolerance. Each returned incumbent
passed chronological replay and the independent saved-decision audit.
The original Gurobi raw CSV is absent from this checkout, although its
metadata, schedules and source archive remain available. Accordingly,
these results are a new CPLEX replication, not a hash-verified paired
comparison of solver runtimes or objective values.
